# TRD-779 Local Review History Inspection

Status: accepted

## Goal

Inspect validated manual local review revisions for one frozen intelligence brief.

## Acceptance

- Local review history is composed only from validated manual local records.
- History records remain aligned to one research case, brief id, and brief hash.
- History inspection is read-only and grants no approval, simulation, execution, export, or dispatch
  authority.
- The web workspace shows saved local revisions without restore, promotion, or external action
  controls.
