# TRD-779 Local Review History Inspection

Status: accepted

## Goal

Retain and inspect validated local manual-review revisions for one frozen intelligence brief.

## Acceptance

- History remains local to the matching frozen brief and content hash.
- Every recovered revision retains hard-false approval, simulation, execution, and external-dispatch
  flags.
- Legacy latest-record storage remains readable.
- The rendered history is read-only and contains no action control.
- Desktop and narrow-view inspection remain free of horizontal overflow.
